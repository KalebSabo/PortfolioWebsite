Eragon Robot

A compact 3D-printed bipedal walker with servo control, balance feedback, and a digital twin in Isaac Sim

Project Overview

Eragon is a compact, mostly 3D-printed bipedal walking robot standing approximately 30 cm (1 ft) tall. Built as an advanced, hands-on engineering platform focusing on servo control, basic gait generation, balance feedback loops, and physical mechanical/electrical integration.

System Architecture

  • Mechanical Design: Custom-designed pelvis chassis and leg geometry optimized for structural integrity and torque distribution.
  • Firmware Architecture: Multithreaded core management via FreeRTOS to decouple time-critical balance adjustments from gait sequences.

Development Phase: MK1 to Digital Twin

Following complete hardware assembly and physical integration of the MK1 platform, the project strategy pivoted toward virtual modeling.

Current Status: Actively transitioning into a Digital Twin simulation space within Isaac Sim — enabling high-fidelity gait training, physics evaluation, and reinforcement learning optimization before flashing updated control blocks back to the physical hardware.
